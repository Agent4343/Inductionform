# Offshore Induction Form - Hebron Platform

## Overview

The Offshore Induction Form template for the Hebron Platform - Green Hat Program has been added to the DigitalFormsApp. This comprehensive checklist ensures all safety and orientation requirements are met for personnel boarding the Hebron offshore platform.

## Form Details

- **Template ID**: `offshore-induction-hebron`
- **Form Reference**: CANE-EC-OFPRO-01-005-4008-00 | 04
- **Category**: Safety
- **Total Items**: 53 checklist items + signature sections

## Form Structure

### 1. Instructions
Form provided to Responsible Supervisor - Copy to be provided to the Onshore Training Administrator via SSH&E Lead. All items to be completed by Heli-admin for all regular employees, vendors and visitors.

### 2. General Orientation (31 items)
Covers essential facility orientation including:
- **Muster Stations**: Locations 1-4 and evacuation systems
- **Living Quarters**: LQ Levels 1-5 facilities and amenities
- **Emergency Procedures**: Alarms (GPA, PAPA), drills, and contact numbers
- **Platform Rules**: PPE requirements, dress code, dietary procedures

### 3. Safety Overview (3 items)
- Hazard identification and personal safety
- Right to work safety
- Process safety & PSMS overview

### 4. Detailed Safety Training (16 items)
Comprehensive safety training including:
- Incident and environmental reporting
- Waste management procedures
- Emergency response duties
- Platform safety systems
- Hazardous materials handling
- PPE requirements and standards

### 5. Work Management System (3 items)
- WMS and PSMS overview
- Control and use of knives procedure
- Mentor appointment and introduction

### 6. Inductee Acknowledgement
Required fields:
- Name (Print)
- Company
- Date
- Inductee Signature ✓ Required

### 7. Supervisor Confirmation
Required fields:
- Offshore Team (e.g., Team A, Team B)
- Responsible Supervisor ✓ Required
- Presenter/Mentor
- Supervisor Signature ✓ Required

## How to Use

1. **Access the Template**:
   - Navigate to Templates in the app
   - Select "Safety" category
   - Choose "Offshore Induction Form - Hebron Platform"

2. **Complete the Form**:
   - Work through each section sequentially
   - Check off completed orientation items
   - Fill in all required text fields
   - Capture required signatures

3. **Export Options**:
   - Save as PDF with company branding
   - Email to stakeholders
   - Submit for approval workflow

## Field Types Used

- **Checkbox**: For all 53 checklist items
- **Text**: For names, company, team designation
- **Date**: For completion date
- **Signature**: For inductee and supervisor signatures
- **Section Headers**: For organizing content into logical groups

## Compliance

This form supports:
- Green Hat Program requirements
- SSH&E (Safety, Security, Health & Environment) protocols
- Offshore platform safety regulations
- Canadian offshore safety standards

## Notes

- All 53 checklist items should be completed before signatures
- Both inductee and supervisor signatures are required
- Form should be provided to Responsible Supervisor
- Copy must be sent to Onshore Training Administrator via SSH&E Lead
- Form is applicable to all personnel: regular employees, vendors, and visitors
